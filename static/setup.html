ATLASfirst-time setup

Welcome to ATLAS

A voice companion that joins your calls, listens, and talks back in a voice you choose. Everything runs on this computer unless you link a cloud model yourself. No accounts, no hidden keys.

Setup takes about five minutes: pick a brain, pick your microphone and speakers, give your agent a voice, and describe who they are.

Choose a brain

The language model that decides what your agent says.

On this computer Free and private. Uses your GPU through Ollama.
Cloud provider Faster on small GPUs. Bring your own API key.

Ollama checking…

Ollama runs local models. Install it, then come back here (this page checks every few seconds).

Your key is stored only on this computer (user/settings.json) and sent only to the provider you pick.

Microphone and speakers

What your agent hears and where it talks. For Discord calls, pick a virtual cable (e.g. VB-Audio / Voicemeeter) on both sides; for a normal chat, your headset is perfect.

Headphones are best: with open speakers the agent may hear itself (echo cancellation helps, but not perfectly).

Give your agent a voice

Upload 5–20 seconds of one person speaking clearly with no music. ATLAS clones the voice locally. Only use voices you have permission to use.

Drop an audio file here or click to choose (wav, mp3, m4a, ogg, flac)

Who is your agent?

Describe them in your own words and ATLAS drafts a personality you can edit. Or import a heart.md character file.

All set

ATLAS will restart once to load your choices, then open the dashboard.

You can change any of this later: the dashboard has a Settings button, and new agents come from the + in the dock.
