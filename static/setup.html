Enable this only for an image-capable API model. Local model support is detected automatically.

Your key is stored only on this computer (user/settings.json) and sent only to the provider you pick.

Microphone and speakers

What your agent hears and where it talks. To join an online voice call (any app), pick a virtual cable (e.g. VB-Audio / Voicemeeter) on both sides; for people in the same room, a mic and speakers are perfect.

Headphones are best: with open speakers the agent may hear itself (echo cancellation helps, but not perfectly).

Give your agent a voice

Upload 5–20 seconds of one person speaking clearly with no music. ATLAS clones the voice locally. Only use voices you have permission to use.

Drop an audio file here or click to choose (wav, mp3, m4a, ogg, flac)

Who is your agent?

Describe them in your own words and ATLAS drafts a personality with the currently loaded model. Edit it freely, or draft again. Or import a heart.md character file.
